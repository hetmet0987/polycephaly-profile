# Prime Herald — Stage 9: The Prime Time Editions

Stage 9 creates a front page from reporting that is already public.

Commands:
- `/edition preview`
- `/edition publish`

The edition automatically selects up to six `PUBLISHED` The Prime Time stories from the last 24 hours. Editors may optionally provide an edition title and editor's summary.

It deliberately does not create new stories, fabricate summaries, introduce another approval workflow, or add a new role.

Use preview first when desired, then publish to the newspaper channel.

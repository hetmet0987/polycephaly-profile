# The Prime Time — Editorial Stylebook v1

## Inspiration without imitation

The Prime Time may learn from major legacy newspapers in:
- strong editorial hierarchy,
- restrained typography,
- headline/deck structure,
- section identity,
- bylines and datelines,
- clear separation between reporting and opinion.

It must retain its own name, visual identity, wording and editorial rules.

## Headline rule

Prefer:
`event + consequence / significance`

Example:
`Prime Kingdom Opens Relations With Three Partners as Foreign Policy Enters a New Phase`

Avoid cheap clickbait:
`YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT PRIME KINGDOM JUST DID`

## Attribution rule

If a proposition is established only by a source, attribute it.

Source:
`Prime Marshal says the exercise met all objectives.`

Preferred headline when not independently established:
`Prime Marshal Says Defence Exercise Met Its Objectives`

Do not silently rewrite it as:
`Defence Exercise Was a Complete Success`

## Headline intensity

Level 1 — Routine
Level 2 — Notable
Level 3 — Major
Level 4 — Breaking
Level 5 — Historic

A newsroom loses authority if every story is BREAKING or HISTORIC.

## Article labels

NEWS — straight reporting.
BREAKING — developing news.
ANALYSIS — interpretation based on established facts.
EXPLAINER — explains a complex system or event.
FEATURE — long-form narrative/context.
INTERVIEW — Q&A/interview format.
EDITORIAL — institutional editorial-board opinion.
OPINION — named author's opinion.

Labels must not be used to disguise opinion as reporting.

## Discord composition

Published article order:
1. THE PRIME TIME masthead
2. Section + canonical PT ID
3. Breaking/Historic treatment only when level requires it
4. Headline
5. Deck
6. Byline
7. Article label + intensity
8. Dateline
9. Body
10. The Prime Time footer

Stage 9 will build full editions/front pages; Stage 2 establishes article-level
visual grammar only.

## Official communications wall

Prime Herald Official Communications:
speaks for the issuing authority.

The Prime Time:
reports, analyzes, explains or comments with an explicit article label.

The newsroom may report an official statement, but the two records remain
separate.
